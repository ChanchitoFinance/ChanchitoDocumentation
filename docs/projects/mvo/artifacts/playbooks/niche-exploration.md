# Go-To-Market Niche Validation Playbook

## 1. Strategic Context

The platform is not validating a single feature or persona — it is validating a **new category**: a *Social Validation Network*.

This introduces three core uncertainties:

1. **Supply-side uncertainty** – who will post ideas and why
2. **Demand-side uncertainty** – who will validate ideas and why
3. **Marketplace dynamics** – whether social + validation loops reinforce each other

The niche strategy exists to **reduce these uncertainties sequentially**, not simultaneously.

---

## 2. The Validation Blocks Framework

All market exploration is structured into **four validation blocks**. Each block isolates a specific risk and learning objective.

### Block A — Founder / Idea Owners (Supply)

Who feels the strongest pain around idea validation and clarity?

Primary risks tested:

* Will users post ideas publicly?
* Do they trust external validation?
* Do they understand and value structured signals?

Key success signals:

* Repeat posting
* Engagement with dashboards
* Response to Pivot Signals

---

### Block B — Validators / Solvers (Demand)

Who enjoys consuming, judging, and reacting to ideas?

Primary risks tested:

* Will users engage without creating ideas?
* Does swipe-based validation feel natural?
* Does reputation and gamification motivate quality feedback?

Key success signals:

* Swipe velocity
* Comment quality
* Retention without creation

---

### Block C — Monetization-Driven Users

Who actually needs **monetization intent signals**, not just feedback?

Primary risks tested:

* Is “I’d pay for it” a meaningful action?
* Are users willing to commit under friction?
* Does the scoring system influence decisions?

Key success signals:

* MIS uplift
* Variant testing usage
* External sharing of results

---

### Block D — Social & Cultural Adoption

Can the platform exist as a **habit-forming social product**, not just a tool?

Primary risks tested:

* Feed stickiness
* Content-as-entertainment
* Identity and taste expression

Key success signals:

* Dwell time
* Passive consumption
* Explore vs. For You usage balance

## 4. How to Use This Document Operationally

### Step 1 — Select 3 Niches Only

At any given time, operate with:

* 1 supply-heavy niche
* 1 demand-heavy niche
* 1 monetization or social niche

Never test more than three concurrently.

---

### Step 2 — Assign a Primary Metric per Niche

Each niche must be tied to **one dominant score**:

* AQS → content clarity & consumption
* DS → interest & demand
* MIS → monetization intent

Secondary metrics are observed, not optimized.

---

### Step 3 — Map Feature Exposure

For each niche, decide:

* Which feed is primary (Explore vs. For You)
* Which actions are emphasized (swipe, comment, follow)
* Which features are hidden

This avoids contaminating signals.

---

### Step 4 — Run 14–30 Day Validation Cycles

Each niche experiment must have:

* A start date
* A kill / pivot threshold
* A single learning objective

No open-ended testing.

---

### Step 5 — Produce a Decision Artifact

Every cycle ends with:

* PASS / HOLD / FAIL
* Primary reason (data-backed)
* Next-best-action recommendation

These outputs feed the **Phase Gates system**.

---

## 5. Executive Decision Rules

* Do not scale niches — scale **validated behaviors**
* Social engagement without signal quality is noise
* Monetization intent without retention is premature
* Emotional comfort is a leading indicator, not a soft metric

## 7. Go-To-Market Assumption Validation Tactics

This section operationalizes the validation blocks through **20 concrete, low-cost go-to-market actions**. These are designed for a **single marketing owner** whose mandate is to *invalidate assumptions*, not to scale acquisition.

Each tactic explicitly maps to one or more validation blocks and produces a **clear signal**, not vanity metrics.

---

### Block A — Founder / Idea Owner Validation (Supply)

**1. Cold Problem-First Founder Interviews**
*Assumption:* Founders feel acute anxiety around idea validation.
**Action:** DM 30 founders with no product mention; ask about their hardest validation challenge.
**Channels:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Indie Hackers
**Signal:** Depth and emotional intensity of responses.

**2. Pain-Only Landing Page**
*Assumption:* The problem resonates before the solution.
**Action:** One-screen landing page describing only the pain and outcome.
**Channels:** Twitter, Reddit, founder Discords
**Signal:** Time on page, email capture rate.

**3. Forced-Choice Founder Waitlist**
*Assumption:* Founders prioritize demand clarity over generic feedback.
**Action:** Waitlist form with a single forced-choice question on top needs.
**Channels:** Landing page, DMs
**Signal:** Clear clustering of needs.

**4. Public Exposure Comfort Test**
*Assumption:* Founders are willing to expose ideas publicly.
**Action:** Post "Would you let strangers tell you if your idea is bad?"
**Channels:** Twitter/X, Indie Hackers
**Signal:** Comment-to-like ratio.

**5. Concierge Validation Offer**
*Assumption:* Founders will trade autonomy for clarity.
**Action:** Manually validate 5 ideas for free.
**Channels:** Slack groups, accelerators
**Signal:** Speed and seriousness of submissions.

---

### Block B — Validator / Solver Validation (Demand)

**6. Swipe-Based Figma Prototype**
*Assumption:* Swipe validation is intuitive.
**Action:** Prototype 5 ideas and observe behavior.
**Channels:** UX communities, Discord
**Signal:** Completion rate and confusion points.

**7. "Judge This Idea" Content Posts**
*Assumption:* Reacting to ideas is intrinsically engaging.
**Action:** Post idea screenshots and prompt judgment.
**Channels:** Twitter/X, Reddit
**Signal:** Comment depth and seriousness.

**8. Validation-as-Entertainment Videos**
*Assumption:* Idea validation works as passive content.
**Action:** Short videos asking viewers to choose ideas.
**Channels:** TikTok, Instagram Reels
**Signal:** Watch time and saves.

**9. Reputation Incentive Test**
*Assumption:* Validators are motivated by recognition.
**Action:** Publicly rank top validators weekly.
**Channels:** Discord, Twitter/X
**Signal:** Repeat participation.

**10. Anonymous Brutality Test**
*Assumption:* Anonymity improves signal quality.
**Action:** Anonymous critique form.
**Channels:** Reddit, Discord
**Signal:** Usefulness vs. toxicity of feedback.

---

### Block C — Monetization Intent Validation

**11. "I'd Pay" Fake-Door CTA**
*Assumption:* Monetization intent is understandable.
**Action:** Add "I'd Pay" button leading to "Coming Soon."
**Channels:** Landing page
**Signal:** Click-through rate.

**12. Pricing Range Poll**
*Assumption:* Users think in price bands.
**Action:** Poll pricing willingness.
**Channels:** Email, Twitter/X
**Signal:** Drop-off beyond key thresholds.

**13. Pre-Order Without Product**
*Assumption:* Some users want commitment, not feedback.
**Action:** Stripe pre-order for a validation report.
**Channels:** DMs, landing page
**Signal:** Conversion and refund rate.

**14. Variant Preference Testing**
*Assumption:* Relative comparison matters more than absolutes.
**Action:** Present two ideas and force a choice.
**Channels:** Email, Discord
**Signal:** Strong preference skew.

**15. Post-Signal Founder Re-Engagement**
*Assumption:* High MIS drives follow-up behavior.
**Action:** Track return behavior after monetization signals.
**Channels:** Product analytics, email
**Signal:** 7-day return rate.

---

### Block D — Social & Cultural Adoption

**16. Curated Idea Feed Newsletter**
*Assumption:* Idea discovery has standalone value.
**Action:** Weekly top-ideas email.
**Channels:** Email/Substack
**Signal:** Open and click rates.

**17. Shareable Validation Cards**
*Assumption:* Results are socially shareable.
**Action:** Generate visual validation summaries.
**Channels:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn
**Signal:** Shares vs. likes.

**18. Lurker Value Analysis**
*Assumption:* Passive users still generate value.
**Action:** Track non-creating user behavior.
**Channels:** Product analytics
**Signal:** Session length and retention.

**19. Cohort-Based Validation Test**
*Assumption:* Group validation increases signal density.
**Action:** Private Discord validation cohort.
**Channels:** Invite-only community
**Signal:** Feedback per idea.

**20. Accelerator Pilot Program**
*Assumption:* Structured environments amplify outcomes.
**Action:** Run a free accelerator pilot.
**Channels:** Direct outreach
**Signal:** Completion of full validation cycles.

---

## 8. Final Note

The objective is not to find *users*.
The objective is to find **repeatable, high-signal behavior loops**.

This document exists to ensure the platform is built on **validated truth, not founder intuition**.
