# **BRAND GUIDELINES — DECISION CLARITY**

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand Name (Working)

**Decision Clarity**

### Category

**Decision Validation Platform**

### Core Positioning

You don’t validate ideas.
You validate decisions.

### One-Sentence Value Proposition

**External signal before irreversible work.**

Alternative (secondary, not headline):
**Make better decisions before you build.**

---

## 2. Brand Philosophy

### What We Believe

* Building is expensive. Deciding wrong is worse.
* Speed without clarity is risk, not progress.
* Founders are not lazy for waiting — they are rational.
* External signal is protection, not permission.
* Good decisions deserve structure, memory, and evidence.

### What We Are Optimizing For

* Reduced regret
* Lower sunk cost
* Fewer irreversible mistakes
* Calm, deliberate progress
* Confidence grounded in signal, not hope

### What We Explicitly Reject

* Hustle theater
* “Just ship it” dogma
* Motivational hype
* Empty engagement metrics
* Loud opinions without evidence

---

## 3. Psychological Contract with the User

### The Implicit Promise

> “We help you decide what to do next — before you commit time, money, or reputation.”

We do **not** promise success.
We promise **clarity, evidence, and reduced uncertainty**.

### Emotional Outcome We Aim For

After using the product, the user should feel:

* Less anxious
* More grounded
* Clearer about tradeoffs
* Comfortable killing or pivoting
* Ready to act *because* they’ve decided

Relief > excitement.

---

## 4. Voice & Language System (Non-Negotiable)

### Tone

* Calm
* Serious
* Adult
* Protective
* Non-performative

### Sentence Style

* Short
* Declarative
* No emojis
* No exclamation marks
* No motivational clichés

### Vocabulary Rules

**Use**

* Decide
* Signal
* Evidence
* Risk
* Commit
* Confidence
* Assumption
* Tradeoff
* Outcome

**Avoid**

* Dream
* Hustle
* Disrupt
* Crush
* 10x
* Magic
* Breakthrough
* Game-changing

### Example Global Copy

* “Before you build, decide.”
* “Get external signal on what to do next.”
* “Reduce risk before committing time, money, or reputation.”
* “Not every idea deserves execution.”
* “Clarity is a competitive advantage.”

---

## 5. Feature Framing Language

Everything in the product must reinforce **decisions as the core object**.

| Feature        | Language Used            |
| -------------- | ------------------------ |
| Votes          | Signals                  |
| Comments       | Evidence                 |
| AI Personas    | Independent perspectives |
| Analytics      | Confidence tracking      |
| Idea Status    | Decision state           |
| Validation     | Risk reduction           |
| External tests | Signal reinforcement     |

Never describe features as “engagement,” “social,” or “fun” in primary copy.

---

## 6. Visual Identity & Design Philosophy

### Design Intent

* High contrast
* Minimal noise
* No decorative UI
* Visual restraint
* Every accent has semantic meaning

The interface should feel like:

> A calm control room, not a social feed.

---

## 7. Color System (Single Source of Truth)

### 7.1 Global Theme

* **Theme Mode:** Dark / premium
* **Base Background:** `#000000`
* **Default Text:** `#FFFFFF`

**Intent:**
Remove distraction. Focus attention. Reduce cognitive load.

---

### 7.2 Accent Colors & Semantic Meaning

#### A) Premium CTA Accent — *Primary Conversion*

* **Color:** `#992BFF`
* **Meaning:** Commitment, money, irreversible action

**Use for**

* “I’d pay for it”
* Upgrade / Checkout / Subscribe
* Monetization badges
* Core conversion states

**Never use for**

* Navigation
* Secondary actions
* Decorative highlights

---

#### B) Primary Accent — *Core UI & Navigation*

* **Color:** `#A07BCF`
* **Meaning:** Interaction, navigation, neutral action

**Use for**

* Sidebar navigation
* Secondary buttons
* Reply links
* Focus rings (non-payment)
* Toggle states

---

#### C) Hover Accent — *Subtle Feedback*

* **Color:** `#B0A7B8`
* **Meaning:** Attention, not action

**Use for**

* Hover borders
* Hover text emphasis
* Dividers on focus

Must never feel like a CTA.

---

#### D) Negative / Risk Signal

* **Color:** `#FF944C`
* **Meaning:** Risk, failure, warning, uncertainty

**Use for**

* Downvotes
* Errors
* Validation failures
* “Low confidence” states
* Destructive actions

---

## 8. Component Behavior Guidelines

### Buttons

**Primary CTA**

* Background: `#992BFF`
* Text: `#FFFFFF`
* Hover: same color, subtle glow

**Secondary**

* Background: transparent / dark surface
* Border or label: `#A07BCF`
* Hover: `#A07BCF`

**Disabled**

* Reduced opacity
* Text still readable

---

### Navigation (Sidebar / Tabs)

* Default: `#FFFFFF`
* Active: `#A07BCF`
* Hover: `#B0A7B8`

Never use premium purple here.

---

### Cards / Lists / Comments

* Default: dark surface on black
* Text: `#FFFFFF`
* Hover border: `#B0A7B8`
* Interactive elements: `#A07BCF`

---

### Voting / Signals

* “I’d pay for it”: `#992BFF`
* Neutral / positive: `#A07BCF`
* Negative: `#FF944C`

These colors must never be repurposed.

---

### Form Fields

* Text: `#FFFFFF`
* Focus ring:

  * Default: `#A07BCF`
  * Payment-related: `#992BFF`
* Error state: `#FF944C`

---

## 9. Brand Guardrails (Hard Rules)

* Never celebrate ideas — only decisions.
* Never push urgency — clarity comes first.
* Never gamify uncertainty.
* Never imply AI is truth.
* Never shame hesitation.
* Never encourage building without signal.

If a design or copy choice increases excitement but reduces clarity, it is wrong.

---

## 10. Strategic Summary

This brand:

* Validates cautious founders
* Creates psychological safety
* Positions decisions as infrastructure
* Builds long-term trust
* Trades virality for credibility
* Trades hype for pricing power

**Decision Clarity is not a tool.**
It is a stance.
